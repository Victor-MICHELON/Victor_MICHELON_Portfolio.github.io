CineMatch AI · Streamlit app · December 2025

A hybrid film recommendation engine

CineMatch AI combines two architectures: content-based filtering, which compares films by what they are (Sentence-BERT embeddings of the plot, topics, cast, origin), and collaborative filtering, which compares them by who liked them. Weights adapt to each film, which solves the cold-start problem: a film with no ratings is still recommended from its content, with the reason why.

×5.6more hits than collaborative filtering on films with no ratings, measured offline (tuned weights)

Deep learning on MRI · November 2025

Brain tumor MRI classification with Grad-CAM

Four classes, a CNN built from scratch against a fine-tuned EfficientNetB0. The score is not the point on its own: Grad-CAM shows which part of the scan drove each prediction, and the maps land where each type of tumor is expected.

97.8%test accuracy, and every prediction shows the brain region behind it
