Recovering the true OFF score in Parkinson’s disease

Predict a patient’s debiased motor score from noisy ON and OFF measurements, rater effects and heavy missingness. Run as a controlled lab of 61 experiments under one locked, patient-grouped protocol, ending in a blend of boosting and sequence networks.

Deep learning on MRI · December 2025

Brain tumor MRI classification with Grad-CAM

Four-class MRI classifier: a CNN baseline against a fine-tuned EfficientNetB0, with Grad-CAM to see what the model looks at, and a reproducible pipeline that hunts near-duplicate leaks between splits.

97.8%test accuracy on 1,311 scans, against 92.1% for the CNN baseline

Classification on seismic data · October 2025

Tsunami risk from seismic data

Can an earthquake’s seismic profile alone flag tsunami risk? A recall-first gradient boosting model, a temporal leak removed, and a geospatial feature: the distance to the nearest tectonic plate boundary.

95%of tsunamis detected on the hold-out set, F1 0.90

About

I study at ESILV in Paris, where I split my time between applied mathematics and software. What I care about is whether a model's answer can be trusted: that its validation is honest, its failures are known, and a person can see why it said what it said.

That is why the projects above show cross-validation protocols, a baseline to beat, Grad-CAM heat maps and a radar of weights. So far my work covers classical machine learning, deep learning, sentence embeddings and agent-assisted workflows with Bob and Claude Code. I am now exploring transformer architectures and MLOps practices.
