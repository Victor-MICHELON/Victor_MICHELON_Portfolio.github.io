IBM × Probabl hackathon · team of three · October 2026

Estimating Parkinson’s motor scores from noisy clinical data

The motor score that best tracks the disease is rarely measured, and the measurements are noisy. We ran the hackathon as a research lab driven by AI agents, with rules that keep them honest: a critic agent hunts for leaks, and a locked protocol judges every experiment the same way.

7th / 295on the Kaggle leaderboard (top 3%), after 61 experiments judged by the same protocol

Streamlit app · December 2025

CineMatch, a recommender that also works on films nobody has rated

Eight signals, from what the plot is about to who made the film, blended with weights that adapt to each film. When a film has no ratings, the engine leans on its content instead of going blind, and every suggestion says why it is there.

Deep learning on MRI · November 2025

Brain tumor MRI classification with Grad-CAM

Four classes, a CNN built from scratch against a fine-tuned EfficientNetB0. The score is not the point on its own: Grad-CAM shows which part of the scan drove each prediction, and the maps land where each type of tumor is expected.

97.8%test accuracy, and every prediction shows the brain region behind it
