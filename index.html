Predicting the unbiased Parkinson's motor score

Bobathon ESILV with IBM and Probabl, October 2026

For each patient visit, estimate the true OFF motor score (MDS-UPDRS) from noisy ON and OFF measurements, rater effects and many missing values. The training set has 44,590 visits from 5,576 patients.

I ran it as a controlled lab, and the chart below plots every run I logged. Every idea was a separate experiment, judged against a reference run fold by fold, and a change only counted when its gain was larger than the spread between folds. A hook locked the raw data and the cross-validation folds so no result could drift.

Limits. The data is synthetic and the scores are cross-validated on the training set. The official Kaggle submission was made separately, and I do not claim a leaderboard rank here.

Python, scikit-learn (HistGradientBoosting), pandas, Claude Code agents for ideas and leakage review

CineMatch, a hybrid movie recommender

Streamlit app, 2025 to 2026

Pick a film and get four similar ones. Plots are turned into sentence embeddings (all-MiniLM-L6-v2), then combined with keyword, genre, cast and director similarity, release context and a collaborative signal.

The weights adapt to the film: horror leans on tone, non-English films on country of origin, blockbusters on audience data. A radar chart shows the weights behind each result, so you can see why a film was suggested. Films with no audience data fall back to content signals.

Limits. I did not measure recommendation quality against ground truth, so there is no accuracy figure. The adaptive weights are rules I set by hand.

Python, sentence-transformers, scikit-learn, Plotly, Streamlit, TMDB data

Brain tumor classification with Grad-CAM

Deep learning on MRI, December 2025

Four classes: glioma, meningioma, pituitary tumor and no tumor. I trained a small CNN from scratch as a baseline, then fine-tuned EfficientNetB0. Grad-CAM overlays show which part of the scan drove each prediction.

On the 1,311 held-out scans, the fine-tuned model reaches 97.8% accuracy against 92.1% for the baseline. Its weakest class is meningioma, with a recall of 0.94.

Limits. One public dataset, one split. Clinical scans from other hospitals would likely score lower, and I have not tested that.

Python, TensorFlow, Keras, scikit-learn

Tsunami risk from earthquake data

Classification on 449 events, October 2025

Can the seismic profile of an earthquake say whether it caused a tsunami? I compared eight models with repeated stratified cross-validation, and gradient boosting won.

Two decisions matter most. Every record before 2012 was a negative case, so the year alone would have leaked the answer, and I removed those rows. I also added a physical feature, the distance from the epicenter to the nearest tectonic plate boundary, computed with GeoPandas.

Limits. The hold-out set has only 90 events, 61 of them tsunamis. A model that always answered "tsunami" would reach a tsunami F1 of 0.81 on that split, so read 0.90 with that in mind. Recall on non-tsunami events is 0.66.

Python, scikit-learn, GeoPandas, Shapely, Seaborn
